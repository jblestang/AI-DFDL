## 1. Overview & Standard Alignment

This refactoring resolved all 3 identified root causes in the expression parser/evaluator, bringing the expression engine into full alignment with DFDL 1.0 (§18, §23) and XPath 2.0 specifications.

### Key Enhancements

1. **Pest PEG Wildcard Step Support** (`dfdl_expression.pest`):
   - Updated `path_step = ${ (".." | "." | "*" | (qname_ident ~ ":")? ~ (qname_ident | "*")) ~ predicate? }`.
   - Enables native parsing of wildcard path expressions (`/*`, `/ex:e1/ex:nest/*`).

2. **Infoset Path Wildcard Matching & Predicate Bracket Stripping** (`types.rs` & `tree.rs`):
   - Updated `InfosetPath::parse` and `try_push` to extract element names cleanly when predicate brackets `[N]` are attached (e.g. `/e2/password[1]`).
   - Updated `InfosetTree::find_element` to support matching wildcard step `*` against child elements.

3. **Dynamic `dfdl:occursIndex()` Tracking** (`eval.rs` & `parser.rs`):
   - Extended `ExprContext` with `occurs_index: usize` and builder method `with_occurs_index`.
   - Updated `ParserEngine` to track `current_occurs_index` during `parse_array` iterations (`1, 2, 3...`) and pass it to `ExprContext` for expression evaluation.
   - Updated `eval_fn_call` for `"occursIndex"` to return `DfdlValue::Long(ctx.occurs_index as i64)`.

4. **Unit Test Suite**:
   - Added unit test assertions in `crates/dfdl-core/src/expr/mod.rs` verifying `dfdl:occursIndex()` index context, wildcard `*` step expression parsing, and predicate path step parsing.
