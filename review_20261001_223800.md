## 2. Architectural Analysis & Standards Conformance

### 2.1 Text Number Normalization (DFDL §13.7, §13.6, §13.2)
- **Problem**:
  - `dfdl:textNumberPattern` prefixes and suffixes (e.g., `'$'#,##0.00;('$'#,##0.00)`, `'begin: '# :end`) and pad characters were not parsed or normalized before scalar parsing.
  - Custom `textStandardDecimalSeparator` (e.g., `^`, `:`, `,`) and `textStandardGroupingSeparator` were ignored in `parse_simple_value` for `xs:decimal`, `xs:integer`, `xs:float`, and `xs:double`.
  - Custom representations for special floating-point values (`textStandardNaNRep`, `textStandardInfinityRep`, `textStandardZeroRep`) were not plumbed into properties or parsing routines.
- **Solution**:
  - Implemented generalized `normalize_text_number` in [`crates/dfdl-core/src/kernel/parser.rs`](file:///Users/jean-baptiste/AI-DFDL/crates/dfdl-core/src/kernel/parser.rs) applied uniformly to all text numeric types (`xs:decimal`, `xs:integer`, `xs:nonNegativeInteger`, `xs:byte`, `xs:short`, `xs:long`, `xs:unsigned*`, `xs:float`, `xs:double`).
  - Added support for `text_standard_nan_rep`, `text_standard_infinity_rep`, and `text_standard_zero_rep` across `ResolvedProperties` in [`crates/dfdl-core/src/schema/ir.rs`](file:///Users/jean-baptiste/AI-DFDL/crates/dfdl-core/src/schema/ir.rs) and property resolution in [`crates/dfdl-core/src/expr/properties.rs`](file:///Users/jean-baptiste/AI-DFDL/crates/dfdl-core/src/expr/properties.rs).
  - Handles pad characters both surrounding and enclosed within affixes (e.g., `****begin: 4 :end`, `(__1)`).

### 2.2 IBM 4690 Packed Decimal Codecs (DFDL §13.7.1.3)
- **Problem**:
  - IBM 4690 packed decimal differs from standard IBM Comp-3 packed decimal: it does not use a trailing sign nibble; instead, negative numbers are indicated by `0xD` at the high nibble of the byte stream, and odd-digit counts are padded with `0xF` at the leftmost nibble(s).
  - Delimited binary reading for packed decimal and BCD was missing boundary-aware scanning.
- **Solution**:
  - Implemented `decode_ibm4690_packed` and `encode_ibm4690_packed` in [`crates/dfdl-core/src/util.rs`](file:///Users/jean-baptiste/AI-DFDL/crates/dfdl-core/src/util.rs).
  - Designed stream-based nibble processing with `checked_mul` and `checked_add` arithmetic to guarantee safe overflow rejection (`DFDLError::arithmetic_overflow`).
  - Integrated `read_binary_rep_bytes` in [`crates/dfdl-core/src/kernel/parser.rs`](file:///Users/jean-baptiste/AI-DFDL/crates/dfdl-core/src/kernel/parser.rs) to support both explicit-length and delimiter-terminated packed values.
  - Connected binary unparsing in [`crates/dfdl-core/src/kernel/unparser.rs`](file:///Users/jean-baptiste/AI-DFDL/crates/dfdl-core/src/kernel/unparser.rs) for `BinaryNumberRep::Packed` and `BinaryNumberRep::Ibm4690Packed`.

### 2.3 Calendar Types Parsing & Pattern Support (DFDL §13.11, §13.8)
- **Problem**:
  - `xs:date`, `xs:dateTime`, and `xs:time` parsing failed when text was padded with `textCalendarPadCharacter` (under `textTrimKind="padChar"`).
  - ICU calendar patterns with custom literal quotes (e.g., `'Today is the 'd'th day of 'MMMM', year 'yyyy`), 2-digit years (`yy`), escaped single quotes (`''`), and multi-language month/day names failed to match.
- **Solution**:
  - Added `parse_calendar_from_text` and `parse_calendar_with_pattern` in [`crates/dfdl-core/src/kernel/parser.rs`](file:///Users/jean-baptiste/AI-DFDL/crates/dfdl-core/src/kernel/parser.rs).
  - Added support for quoting and escaped single quotes (`''`), week symbols (`w`/`W`), time zone offsets, AM/PM markers (`a`), and ERA markers (`G`).
  - Expanded localized month and day dictionaries (English, Spanish, German, Russian) without external crate bloat, preserving `#![no_std]` + `alloc` compatibility.

## 3. Domain Reviews

### 3.1 Binary Codec & Safety Review
- **Arithmetic Safety**: Every digit accumulation step uses Rust's `checked_mul(10)` and `checked_add(digit)` returning typed `DFDLError::arithmetic_overflow`. Zero panics can occur on invalid bit patterns or out-of-range integer representations.
- **Slice Access Safety**: All bitstream and slice accesses use boundary checks (`get_checked`, `get_slice_checked`, `strip_prefix`, `strip_suffix`), eliminating out-of-bounds indexing.
- **`#![no_std]` + `alloc` Preservation**: All changes strictly rely on `alloc::{string::String, vec::Vec}` and core integer primitives, maintaining embedded/Wasm compatibility.

### 3.2 DFDL Spec Conformance Review
- Conforms to DFDL Specification v1.0 Sections:
  - §13.2 (`textTrimKind`, `textPadKind`, pad characters)
  - §13.6 (`textStandardDecimalSeparator`, `textStandardGroupingSeparator`)
  - §13.7 (`textNumberPattern`, `textStandardNaNRep`, `textStandardInfinityRep`, `textStandardZeroRep`)
  - §13.7.1.3 (`binaryNumberRep="ibm4690Packed"`)
  - §13.11 (`calendarPattern`, `calendarPatternKind`, `textCalendarPadCharacter`)
