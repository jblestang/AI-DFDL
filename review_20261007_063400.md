## 1. Executive Summary & Defect Scope (Issue F7)

### 1.1 Specification Baseline (DFDL v1.0 §16.1.4 / GFD.240 page 173)
- Under `dfdl:occursCountKind="parsed"`, occurrences are determined solely through speculative parsing until a processing error occurs.
- The specification explicitly dictates that when parsing, the occurrence count is determined without reference to `minOccurs` or `maxOccurs`.
- When schema validation is enabled (`ValidationMode::Limited` or `ValidationMode::Full`), occurrences fewer than `minOccurs` or exceeding `maxOccurs` are flagged as **Validation Errors** (`DFDLErrorKind::Validation`), not *Processing Errors* (`DFDLErrorKind::Parse`).
- Importantly, validation errors in DFDL do not abort parsing of the enclosing sequence or choice; the parse tree is constructed first, and validation diagnostics are emitted when validation mode is enabled.
