## 1. Executive Summary & Root Cause Resolutions

In accordance with DFDL 1.0 Specification (§5.2, §13.7, §15.2, §15.4), systematic architectural fixes were implemented to resolve **Expected Error Not Raised** issues and improve error classification:

1. **Detailed Facet Restriction Validation (`crates/dfdl-core/src/schema/ir.rs` & `crates/dfdl-core/src/kernel/parser.rs`):**
   - Implemented `SimpleTypeFacets::validate_value_detailed(&self, val: &DfdlValue) -> DFDLResult<()>` to check `minInclusive`, `maxInclusive`, `minExclusive`, `maxExclusive`, `pattern`, `enumeration`, `minLength`, and `maxLength`.
   - Integrated `validate_value_detailed` into `parse_simple_value` during parsing, immediately returning a `DFDLErrorKind::Validation` with exact diagnostic messages (e.g., `Validation Error: maxInclusive 5.0`).

2. **Binary Number Representation (`binaryNumberRep`) Codecs (`crates/dfdl-core/src/schema/ir.rs`, `properties.rs`, `parser.rs`):**
   - Added `BinaryNumberRep` (`Binary`, `Packed`, `Bcd`, `Ibm4690Packed`) to `ResolvedProperties`.
   - Updated `parse_binary_value` to decode IBM Comp-3 packed decimal and BCD values, raising standard BCD nibble errors (`not an allowed type for bcd`).

3. **Assertion Diagnostic String Formatting (`crates/dfdl-core/src/kernel/parser.rs`):**
   - Updated assertion failure formatting to include test expressions (e.g. `Assertion failed for dfdl:checkConstraints(.)`), matching TDML test expectation assertions.
