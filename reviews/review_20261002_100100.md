## 1. Domain Expertise Analysis & Conformance Framework

### A. Mainframe & Midrange Numerical Encoding (DFDL Spec §13.7.2, §13.7.3)
In legacy telecommunication and COBOL transactional systems (IBM System/360 through z/Architecture, Tandem NonStop, HP/CA-Realia), numeric representation relies heavily on zoned decimals (overpunch numbers) where sign encoding shares the nibble of the leading or trailing digit.
- **`asciiStandard`**: Positive digits `{` (0), `A`..`I` (1..9); negative digits `}` (0), `J`..`R` (1..9).
- **`asciiTranslatedEbcdic`**: When ASCII-translated from EBCDIC CP037/IBM037 or parsed directly on EBCDIC channels.
- **`asciiCaRealiaModified`**: Negative digits overpunched with space `' '` for 0, and `'!'`..`')'` for digits 1..9.
- **`asciiTandemModified`**: High-bit overpunch encoding `0x80` for 0, `0x81`..`0x89` for 1..9.
- **Virtual Decimal Point (`V` / `v`)**: In COBOL `PIC 9(3)V99` declarations, decimals do not physically encode a radix character (`.` or `,`). The position of `V` determines the fractional scale. Spec §13.7.2 prohibits `#` after `V` and forbids multiple subpatterns separated by `;` in zoned patterns.
- **Type Applicability**: Per DFDL §13.7.2, zoned numbers are strictly applicable to `xs:decimal` and all integer types (`xs:integer`, `xs:int`, `xs:long`, `xs:short`, `xs:byte`, `xs:unsignedLong`, etc.). Any attempt to declare or parse `xs:float` or `xs:double` with zoned representation triggers a Schema Definition Error.

### B. Internationalization & ICU Calendar Rollover (DFDL §13.12, §13.13)
- **Lax Calendar Policy (`calendarCheckPolicy="lax"`)**: In DFDL, `calendarCheckPolicy="lax"` is the normative default. Date and time fields that overflow standard month/day/hour/minute ranges (e.g., day 45 in month 12, hour 27, minute 61) undergo proleptic calendar rollover through Gregorian epoch calculations rather than failing.
- **Day of Week Resolution (`'e'`, `'E'`, `calendarFirstDayOfWeek`)**: When a pattern specifies a day of week without a day of month (e.g. `yyyy-MM-e` or `EEEE MMM yyyy`), ICU Calendar selects the first day of that month matching the specified day-of-week, parameterized by `calendarFirstDayOfWeek` (`Sunday`, `Monday`, etc.).
- **Greedy Natural Language Token Matching**: Multi-lingual calendar parsing (e.g. Spanish `"Noviembre"` vs `"Nov"`, or Russian/German month names) requires greedy prefix resolution so that shorter abbreviation tokens do not shadow full month and weekday names.

### C. Binary and Calendar Stream Framing
- **Delimited Binary Terminators**: For `representation="binary"` with `lengthKind="delimited"`, variable-length binary payloads (packed decimals, BCD, IBM 4690, binary calendars) must stop immediately before the element's enclosing `terminator` or in-scope sequence/choice delimiter, without consuming delimiter bits from the bitstream.
