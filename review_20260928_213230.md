## 1. Domain & Specification Conformance (DFDL 1.0 §15 Choices & Discriminators)
- **Choice Model Groups (`<xs:choice>`)**:
  - `ParserEngine` attempts choice branches sequentially until a branch succeeds or a discriminator commits.
  - `UnparserEngine` inspects infoset child elements (`find_child_elements`) to match and serialize the active choice branch.
- **Speculative Rollback & Backtracking**:
  - On choice branch failure or discriminator rejection, reader state (`reader.rollback()`) and infoset tree state (`builder.rollback()`) are restored cleanly without stream corruption or partial infoset tree leaking.
- **Discriminator Expression Engine (`dfdl:discriminator="{...}"`)**:
  - Choice branches evaluate discriminator expressions dynamically using `eval_expr`.
  - Disqualified choice branches (discriminator evaluates to `false`) trigger immediate backtracking.
