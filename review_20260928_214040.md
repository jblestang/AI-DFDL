## 1. Domain-Specific Analysis & Assessment

### A. DFDL 1.0 Compliance & Spec Alignment (§12, §13)
- **Bit/Byte Alignment**: Added `AlignmentUnits` enum (`Bytes`, `Bits`) to `ir.rs`. `ParserEngine::parse_term` and `UnparserEngine::unparse_term` accurately calculate bit boundary alignments (`alignment * 8` for `Bytes`, `alignment` for `Bits`). Padding in unparsing writes bits using `fill_byte`.
- **Text Trimming Strategy**: Added `TextTrimKind` enum (`None`, `Head`, `Tail`, `Both`) and `text_pad_char` string to `ResolvedProperties`. `ParserEngine::parse_text_value` trims specified pad characters (`Head`, `Tail`, or `Both`) prior to scalar type parsing.
- **Explicit Text Padding**: `UnparserEngine::unparse_text_value` checks `LengthKind::Explicit` and target string byte length, inserting `text_pad_char` padding (head or tail) to meet exact field width requirements.

### B. Safety, Memory, & `no_std` + `alloc` Verification
- **Panic-Free Runtime**: Zero `unwrap()`, `expect()`, `todo!()`, or unchecked indexing added.
- **Checked Calculations**: Alignment offsets use `saturating_mul`, modulo arithmetic, and overflow-checked bit positions.
- **Budget Control**: `parse_term` and `unparse_term` preserve `WorkBudget` bounds checking.
